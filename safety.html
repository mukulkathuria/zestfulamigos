Safety is Not a Feature —

Every decision at Zestful Amigos is made with the safety of both users and companions at its core. Here's exactly what we do.

Protection System

Six Layers of Safety

Live Location Sharing

From the moment a session begins to the moment it ends, both the user and companion share their real-time GPS location with Zestful Amigos's monitoring team. Neither party shares location directly with each other — it goes to us. Any deviation from expected patterns triggers an automatic check-in.

SOS Emergency Button

Both parties have a one-tap SOS button visible at all times during an active session. Pressing it immediately alerts our emergency response team, sends your live location, and notifies up to 3 pre-set emergency contacts. Response is guaranteed within 90 seconds.

24/7 Monitoring Team

Our dedicated safety team works around the clock. They monitor active sessions, respond to SOS alerts, and are empowered to contact local authorities or dispatch assistance within minutes of a confirmed emergency.

Curated Safe Meeting Points

We never suggest private locations for first meets. Our app provides a curated list of public, well-lit, camera-monitored venues in your city — partner cafés, malls, and parks. You choose from our approved list only.

Encrypted, Monitored Chat

All in-app messages are end-to-end encrypted between users. Our AI moderation layer flags inappropriate content, threats, or requests that violate policy. Flagged conversations are reviewed by a human moderator within 1 hour.

Strict Zero-Tolerance Policy

Any companion or user reported for harassment, inappropriate requests, or policy violations is immediately suspended pending investigation. Verified violations result in permanent bans and, where warranted, reports to law enforcement.

SOS EMERGENCY
Emergency Response

The SOS Button — Built for the Worst Moments

We hope you never need it. But it's there, always visible, always one tap away. Here's what happens when it's pressed:

Your live GPS location is immediately captured and logged.
An alert is sent to our 24/7 emergency response team within 5 seconds.
Up to 3 pre-set emergency contacts receive an SMS with your location.
A team member calls you within 90 seconds to assess the situation.
If needed, police or emergency services are dispatched immediately.
Session is automatically paused and the other party is notified of a safety hold.
Companion Verification

No One Gets Listed Unvetted

Every companion goes through a rigorous multi-step verification before they appear on the platform.

01

Government ID Verification

Aadhaar, PAN, or Passport verified via third-party KYC provider.

02

Photo & Selfie Match

Live selfie matched against ID photo using facial recognition.

03

Background Check

Police clearance certificate verified. Any criminal history results in immediate rejection.

04

Safety Interview

A video call with a Zestful Amigos team member to review conduct expectations and guidelines.

05

Approval & Listing

Profile goes live only after all checks pass. The verified badge appears on every listing.
