Our Story

We Believe in the
Power of Presence

Zestful Amigos was founded on a simple truth — human connection matters, and no one should feel alone when they don't have to be.

Our Mission

Redefining Companionship for the Modern World

In a world that's more connected digitally yet increasingly lonely in person, we built Zestful Amigos to bridge that gap. Whether you're new to a city, planning a birthday without a plus-one, or simply want someone to share a meal with — you deserve that experience.

We're not a dating app. We're a companionship platform — a professional service built around safety, transparency, and genuine human connection. Every booking is secured, every companion verified, and every interaction monitored for both parties' peace of mind.

Our model is simple: we provide the infrastructure, the trust layer, and the safety net. You bring the intention. Together, we make moments better.

Our Values

What We Stand For

Safety First

Every feature we build, every policy we write — safety is the non-negotiable first principle. SOS alerts, live location, vetted companions. Always.

Radical Transparency

Pricing is clear, policies are plain-language, and our 5% fee is the only cost we take. No hidden charges, no surprises.

Dignity & Respect

Companions are professionals. Every user, every companion deserves dignity — we have zero tolerance for harassment or misconduct.

Privacy Protected

Your personal data belongs to you. We collect only what's necessary, store it securely, and never sell it to third parties.

Inclusive by Design

Zestful Amigos is for everyone — all genders, all backgrounds, all reasons. Connection has no demographic.

Continuous Improvement

We listen, iterate, and evolve. Community feedback shapes our roadmap — you're not just a user, you're a co-creator.
