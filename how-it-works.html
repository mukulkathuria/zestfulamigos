The Process

From Search to Meet —
Every Step Guided

We've designed each step of Zestful Amigos to be simple, transparent, and safe — for users and companions alike.

Step 01

Search for a Companion

Open the Zestful Amigos app and browse verified companions in your city. You can filter by gender, interests, languages spoken, availability, and hourly rate. Every profile shows verified photo, ratings, and reviews from past users.

📍 Location-based discovery
Step 02

Choose Your Time & Book

Select your preferred date, time slot, and duration (minimum 1 hour). Write a brief note about the occasion — a birthday, a dinner, a walk in the park. Payment is collected securely and held in escrow until the session completes.

💳 Escrow payment — released after session
Step 03

Companion Receives Notification

Your selected companion gets an instant notification with your booking details. They have 15 minutes to accept or decline. If declined, you're offered alternatives immediately and your payment is not charged.

⏱ 15-minute acceptance window
Step 04

Connect via In-App Chat

Once confirmed, you can message your companion through our end-to-end encrypted in-app chat. Discuss the plan, ask any questions, and coordinate. All messages are retained for safety purposes.

🔒 E2E encrypted messaging
Step 05

Meet at a Safe Location

Our app suggests curated public meeting spots — cafés, parks, malls — in your city. Both parties share live location with the Zestful Amigos safety team for the duration of the session. No need to share personal addresses.

📡 Live location — shared with safety team
Step 06

Session Complete — Rate & Review

At session end, both parties confirm completion in the app. Payment is released to the companion (95%), and we retain 5%. Both parties rate each other — this mutual rating system keeps our community high-quality.

⭐ Mutual rating system
